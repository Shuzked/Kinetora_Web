    Our Story

    Kinetora was born out of a simple observation: most startups fail not because of their technology, but because they fail to communicate their value visually. In the high-stakes world of B2B SaaS and Fintech, your interface is your resume. If it looks amateur, you've lost before the first meeting.

    Our founder, Rafael Muñoz, spent years at the intersection of design and engineering, witnessing how 'creative friction' destroyed momentum. Traditional agencies were too slow; freelancers were too inconsistent. Startups needed a partner that operated at their speed — 48-hour turnarounds, no-BS communication, and absolute visual precision.

    Our Mission

    Today, Kinetora is the strategic partner for Series A and B startups worldwide. We don't just 'make things pretty'. We engineer visual systems that investors trust and users love. From our headquarters in Spain, we've helped companies like Elixir Games and LitLab raise over $14.2M by translating complex technical roadmaps into compelling visual narratives.
